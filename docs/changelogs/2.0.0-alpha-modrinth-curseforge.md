# Changelog - 2.0.0-alpha

This alpha brings five tiers of Umbrafall meteorites, three crater encounters, and cooperative Moonwounds to Cobblemon 1.8.4. Trainers can build an Umbra Infuser, teach Shadow moves with 18 new TMs, and choose rules inspired by Colosseum, XD, or Pokémon GO.

**Upgrading from 1.0.6** — Use Minecraft 1.21.1 and Java 21. Back up your world and configs before opening them in the alpha. Existing Shadowfall Meteoroid blocks and item stacks load as Umbrafall Shell. New sites provide the layered terrain and encounters. Update command blocks and datapacks that use the renamed commands listed below.

## New Features

*   **World Generation: Umbrafall**:
    *   Rebuilt meteoroids into five tiers: **Faint, Weak, Moderate, Strong, and Resonant**, with varied shapes, layered interiors, mineral deposits, and scattered debris.
    *   Added new materials and building blocks, including **Shadow Dust, Refined Shadow Shards, Raw Meteoric Alloy, Condensed Shadow, and Core Samples**.
    *   Craft **Shadow Signal Data** to locate a matching Umbrafall or generate one nearby. The Aura Reader guides you to the site.
    *   Your first **Faint** site gives a gathering hint. Break **Shadow Residue** on the crater floor by hand to collect **Shadow Dust**.

*   **Umbrafall Field Encounters**
    *   Each **Weak** or **Moderate** site rolls once, with a **35%** chance by default to host one of three encounters. The Pokémon stay close to their starting spots in the crater or on the meteorite. The first trainer to start a battle claims the encounter. If that trainer leaves for two minutes, someone else can finish it.
    *   **Cornered at the Crater** lets you rescue a wild Pokémon by defeating or catching its three Shadow attackers. Choose **Welcome** to spend a spare Poké Ball and keep it. If you let it go or have no eligible ball, it leaves you Oran Berries.
    *   In **The Outbreak**, the pack scatters when you come within four blocks of a visible member. Watch which way they run. Flecks on the ground point toward the unmarked fragment carrier’s refuge. Catch or defeat the carrier to earn Shadow Dust.
    *   In **Answering the Howl**, a lone Shadow Pokémon that finishes a turn below half HP can cry for help. You have one turn to catch it, knock it out, or interrupt the call with a major status condition. A Pokémon that answers waits at the crater for a second battle, or joins the caller mid-battle with **SOS Encounters**. Finishing either route earns a Joy Scent.

*   **Moonwound Encounters**:
    *   Added opt-in encounters for **1–4 players** at eligible Strong and Resonant Umbrafalls.
    *   Excavate three layers, battle waves of uncatchable **Void Shadows**, and vote to **Harvest**, **Seal**, or **Overload** the core.
    *   Overload leads to an **Alpha Shadow Pokémon** encounter. With the supported **Raid Dens** integration installed, Resonant sites can instead culminate in a shared-health Shadow raid.
    *   Moonwound party members can join their raid battle late and contribute damage to the shared boss.
    *   Added personal loot rewards, party readiness controls, objective tracking, and bonuses for quick excavation and battles.
    *   Added animated Moonwound portals with **Smooth, Pixelated, and Chunky** visual styles.

*   **Umbra Infuser**:
    *   Added a two-block machine for infusing items and converting eligible owned Pokémon into Shadow Pokémon.
    *   Added connected **Umbra Energy Tanks** to expand charge storage.
    *   Added infusion recipes for specialized Poké Balls, Shadow Scales, held items, and recovery supplies.

*   **New Poké Balls**:
    *   Added **Penumbra, Antumbra, and Umbra Balls**, with improved Shadow capture performance and configurable corruption chances.
    *   Added the craftable **Dark Ball**, which turns eligible captured Pokémon into Shadow Pokémon. By default, it grants a one-time **5-level boost**, respecting level caps, and starts the Pokémon with **twice its species' normal Heart Gauge maximum**. Both values are server-configurable.
    *   All four balls use Cobblemon’s Poké Ball and tier tags so recipes and other mods can recognize them.

*   **Shadow Moves & Equipment**:
    *   Added **18 native Shadow TMs** for replacing an active Shadow move through Cobblemon's TM system while preserving remaining PP and PP Ups.
    *   Datapacks can give each species a **Shadow learnset** with level-up moves, TM eligibility, and moves it can keep after purification. Encounter authors can set an individual Pokémon’s exact starting moves with `sh_shadow_moves`.
    *   Added **Shadow Scales** for randomly replacing a selected Shadow move or swapping an unlocked ordinary move with an eligible benched move.
    *   Added **Shadow Gem, Riven Orb, Shadow Collar, Dissonance Band, Breach Lens, EM Wave Antenna, Ruin Policy, Calming Sachet, Heart Soothe Bell, and Null Charm** for new battle and care options.
    *   Added **Bitter Reawakening**, which revives a fainted Shadow Pokémon outside battle to **75% HP** in exchange for reclosing part of its Heart Gauge.

*   **Progression & Rewards**:
    *   Expanded the main advancement tree to **32 entries**, guiding players through Shadow discovery, purification, equipment, infusion, and Moonwounds.
    *   Added the **Purity Ribbon** for Pokémon protected from Shadow corruption by a Purified Gem.

*   **Trainer & NPC Management**:
    *   Added an in-game operator editor with a searchable trainer/NPC roster, Shadow settings, presets, weighted Pokémon pools, and a review-before-save workflow.
    *   Access the editor through **`/sh npc edit`** or the mod's configuration screen. Compatible NPC integrations and RCT trainer rules are supported.

*   **Gameplay Presets & Config Snapshots**:
    *   Added **Colosseum-inspired, XD-inspired, Pokémon GO-style, and Shadowed Hearts defaults** gameplay presets, with change previews for world owners and server operators.
    *   Presets apply editable settings once; no preset activates automatically. The defaults preset resets only the settings covered by the presets.
    *   Added a **Snapshots** tab for named saves, automatic recovery history, change previews, and restore/delete controls for local preferences and server gameplay/world settings.
    *   Each config keeps up to **100 named snapshots** and its latest **20 automatic snapshots**. Preset changes and restores preserve a recovery point, so a restore can itself be undone.
    *   Snapshots restore settings only; existing Pokémon, inventories, saved progression, and generated terrain are not rolled back.

## Improvements & Refinement

*   **Aura Reader Overhaul**:
    *   Rebuilt the Reader around a **batteryless visor** with Shadow contact pulses, focused analysis, target pinning, and Umbrafall signal navigation.
    *   Added a first-use tutorial, configurable HUD scaling, and reduced-motion settings.
    *   Added temporary battle analysis and timed locks that protect eligible wild Shadow Pokémon from natural despawning while the lock remains valid.
    *   Removed the unused **Direction Arrow** item (`shadowedhearts:direction_arrow`). The visor draws its compass markers directly.

*   **Snag Machines**:
    *   Removed energy requirements from both machine tiers.
    *   By default, **Prototype** requires one intervening battle turn between valid snag attempts, while **Advanced** can attempt a snag on consecutive turns.
    *   Advanced's default bonuses are a **1.25× catch-rate multiplier** for eligible NPC Shadow snags and removal of **10% of the captured Pokémon's remaining Heart Gauge**.
    *   Added independent turn-spacing, catch-multiplier, and capture gauge-reduction settings for each tier.
    *   Added optional **XD-style snag delivery**: captured NPC Pokémon join the party or PC only after the capturing side wins the battle.
    *   Both machines still require arming for each throw and allow at most one snag attempt per player per battle turn.

*   **Purification & Care**:
    *   Rebalanced all **15 scent recipes** around native Campfire Pot brewing, consistent ingredient tiers, and **two-dose batches**.
    *   Improved the Purification Chamber with an interactive tutorial, clearer matchup feedback, and layouts that fit smaller windows.
    *   Expanded Heart Gauge settings with separate reveal thresholds for moves, nature, level/EXP, IVs, and EVs.
    *   The first ordinary move normally unlocks at **80% Heart Gauge remaining**. A Pokémon given a starting set with no Shadow moves can use its first ordinary move at full gauge.
    *   Added optional Colosseum/XD nature-based care tables, persistent per-Pokémon walking progress, and original-roster Heart Gauge capacity profiles.

*   **Game-inspired Battle Rules**:
    *   Added configurable Colosseum-style Shadow Rush recoil and Hyper Mode behavior, XD species-based Reverse Mode entry, and unlimited PP for eligible Shadow moves.
    *   Added an option to retain assigned Shadow moves until purification, followed by native ordinary move restoration.
    *   Added opt-in **GO-style Frustration** on future corruption and **Return** on purification, with a server-controlled window for replacing Frustration. Purification still grants Return if Frustration was removed during that window.

*   **Visuals & Customization**:
    *   Added **Signature, Colosseum, and XD** Shadow aura styles, with a configurable server default. The **Random** server setting gives each Pokémon a consistent randomly selected style.
    *   Added a **Performance** rendering option for all three aura styles in the world and in menus. Signature also offers **Smooth**, **Pixelated** (default), and **Chunky** settings.
    *   Added **Colosseum Token, Gale Phial, Umbrafall Prism, and Agate Dew** for choosing or resetting an owned Pokémon's cosmetic aura style.
    *   Added Snag throw, capture, and shake effects, shadowy trails for the infused ball family, and an anime-inspired Dark Ball capture effect with quality settings.
    *   Added **Reduced Motion, Reduced Flashes, and Minimal** presentation presets, with individual effect overrides.
    *   Added Cobblemon-style preset/snapshot controls and confirmation dialogs, compact layouts, and snapshot timestamps.

*   **Sound**:
    *   Added Moonwound opening and ambient audio with a timed crossfade, Signal Data search audio, and positional Void Shadow transformation sounds.
    *   Added separate volume controls for Moonwound opening/ambience, Signal Data searches, and Void Shadow transformations.

*   **Recipes & Configuration**:
    *   Added optional **JEI, REI, and EMI** support for Umbra Infuser recipes and fuel information.
    *   On Fabric and NeoForge, you can browse settings by topic or search by name. The optional YACL editor also supports precise decimal values and list editing.
    *   Added server controls for Moonwound excavation targets, wave sizes, opponent levels, recovery timers, and pace bonuses.
    *   Server owners can tune field encounter chances and weights, rescue and outbreak pack sizes, the rescue timer, scatter distance, and rewards. Casts can come from biome-specific datapacks or Cobblemon’s local spawn pool, with an optional datapack fallback.
    *   Expanded controls for individual item effects, Shadow TM teaching, purification pacing, and Infuser eligibility and costs.
    *   Added a **locate-only Signal Data mode** for finding existing Umbrafall sites without generating new ones.

## Bug Fixes

*   **Battle & Shadow Logic**:
    *   Corrected Reverse Mode entry timing to follow the acting Pokémon's move.
    *   Fixed Reverse Mode persisting after a Pokémon faints.
    *   Fixed Call actions sometimes failing to reduce Heart Gauge.
    *   Call can clear a saved Hyper or Reverse Mode state even after the server disables that mode.
    *   Fixed canceled Shadow capture attempts leaving battles waiting for the capture to finish.
    *   Fixed fractional wild Shadow spawn chances being rounded to whole percentages.
    *   Fixed Shadow auras failing to appear on newly spawned Pokémon or after corruption in the world.
    *   Restored Cobblemon’s normal following behavior when a trainer sends out a Pokémon.
    *   Fixed authored Shadow chance properties rolling repeatedly during Pokémon creation, and corrected initial Heart Gauge calculation for authored species and overrides.
    *   Corrected Shadow Down's targeting and Protect handling, Shadow Half's per-target protection and damage registration, Shadow Blast's critical-hit stage, and Shadow Sky damage during Dig/Dive.

*   **Purification & Item Use**:
    *   The Relic Stone rounds its remaining cooldown up to the next whole second so the display stays above zero until the stone is ready.
    *   Fixed partial Purification Chamber walking progress being lost across saves and reloads.
    *   Corrected buffered EXP and EV handling, including native training limits, item consumption, and configured RCT level caps.
    *   Scents now consume a dose and start their cooldown only when they reduce Heart Gauge or perform enabled Hyper/Reverse Mode calming.
    *   Tightened server validation and synchronization for chamber access, Pokémon transfers, snag attempts, and item use.

*   **UI & Encounters**:
    *   Corrected the Shadowed Hearts name in English stencil diagnostics.
    *   Fixed Aura Reader targeting, signal cycling, and selected-site persistence across reconnects.
    *   Fixed an Umbra Infuser screen crash and improved machine interfaces at smaller window sizes and higher GUI scales.
    *   Fixed a crash when opening the optional YACL settings screen in the Fabric release.
    *   Improved Moonwound recovery after interrupted sessions and corrected raid-boss placement around large meteorites.
    *   Fixed damaged automatic config snapshots preventing access to healthy recovery points.

## Technical Changes

*   **Dependencies & Compatibility**:
    *   Updated the target to **Cobblemon 1.8.4 on Minecraft 1.21.1**, with Fabric and NeoForge builds.
    *   Fabric requires **Loader 0.19.5**, **Fabric API 0.116.17+1.21.1**, **Fabric Language Kotlin 1.14.2+kotlin.2.4.21**, and **Forge Config API Port 21.1.6** or newer for Minecraft 1.21.1.
    *   Removed the **Special Model Loader** dependency on Fabric. Shadowed Hearts now loads the Relic Stone’s OBJ model directly.
    *   Raised NeoForge minimums to **21.1.259** and **Kotlin for Forge 5.12.0**. Both loaders require **Architectury 13.0.11** or newer.
    *   Aligned declared optional dependency minimums with **Accessories 1.1.0-beta.54+1.21.1**, **REI 16.0.799**, **EMI 1.1.24+1.21.1**, and Fabric's **Mod Menu 11.0.5**.
    *   Updated the optional **FTB Chunks** integration target to **2101.1.23** with **FTB Library 2101.1.39**.
    *   Updated to **Mega Showdown 1.3.3** and expanded support with Shadowium-Z, Shadow Supernova, and configurable Shadow battle-gimmick permissions.
    *   Added optional **Heart Gauge scaling** for the existing Pokémon GO-style Shadow damage modifiers. Damage modifiers and scaling are disabled by default.
    *   Resonant Moonwound raids use **Raid Dens 0.14.1+1.21.1** with **Asymmetric Battles API 1.4.1+1.21.1**.
    *   **Horde Encounters 1.2.2** can bring nearby rescue attackers or outbreak pack members into one battle when herd battles are enabled.
    *   **Answering the Howl** supports **SOS Encounters 1.3.0** with **Raid Dens 0.13.5 or 0.14.1** on Fabric and NeoForge. Fixed a crash on 0.13.5 when SOS reserved a slot for the Pokémon answering the call.
    *   Restored support for **Cobblemon Battle Extras 1.13.47** on **Fabric and NeoForge**. Its battle screens hide locked moves and show Shadow move icons and type matchups.
    *   With **Cobblemon Mid-Battle EXP 2.0.0** installed, move-learning prompts filter out moves a Shadow Pokémon cannot learn. The server also protects locked moves when a player confirms a replacement.
    *   Removed the dedicated **Cobblemon Party Extras** integration.

*   **Configuration & Data**:
    *   Reorganized settings into client presentation, gameplay/snagging, and world-generation/server files, with automatic migration and backups.
    *   Added datapack-controlled infusion recipes and Moonwound loot tables.
    *   Added claim-boundary checks for Infuser energy networks when **FTB Chunks** is installed.
    *   Added **`/sh rules list|preview|apply`** and **`/sh rules snapshots <gameplay|world> list|save|preview|restore|delete`** for server operators and the console.

*   **Command Changes**:
    *   Made **`/sh shadowify`** targets explicit with **`entity`**, **`party`**, or **`slot`**; explicit Heart Gauge values now use the **`gauge`** keyword.
    *   Moved **`steps`**, **`partysteps`**, and **`voidshadow spawn`** under **`/sh debug`**.
    *   Consolidated **`/sh npc preview`** into **`/sh npc inspect`** and **`/sh npc tag preset`** into **`/sh npc preset`**.
    *   Removed **`/sh reloadConfigs`**; settings use the native configuration editor and loader reload lifecycle.
